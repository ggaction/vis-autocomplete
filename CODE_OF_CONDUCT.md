# Code of Conduct

We want people with different backgrounds and levels of experience to be able to contribute to VisAutocomplete. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, project discussions, and other spaces where people represent the project.

## Working together

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique behavior and code rather than a person's identity or competence.
- Welcome questions and help people understand decisions. Good intentions do not remove the need to listen and correct mistakes.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and other people's time. Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence and the task.
- Do not harass, threaten, insult, discriminate against, or sexually objectify others. Do not publish private information without permission or repeatedly contact someone who has asked you to stop.

## Reporting a concern

Contact Hyeon Jeon privately at [jeonhyun97@gmail.com](mailto:jeonhyun97@gmail.com). Include links or context and any immediate safety concern. Please do not post sensitive reports in a public issue. If the report concerns the listed contact, you may use GitHub's platform reporting tools instead.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical; information may need to be shared with people responsible for investigating or addressing the concern. We cannot promise absolute confidentiality.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, ask for a change in behavior, pause a discussion, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. The response should reflect the behavior, its impact, and any pattern of repeated conduct. People involved should receive an explanation when doing so is safe and appropriate. To request reconsideration, contact the reporting address with relevant new information.
