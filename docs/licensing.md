## What this license does not cover

- The ggaction and transition submodules retain their own MIT licenses.
- npm packages, native libraries, fonts, and other third-party components retain their original licenses.
- Example data, corpus chart specifications, and corpus-derived material do not receive a blanket Apache-2.0 grant just because they are stored here. Preserve and inspect the source records before redistributing them or incorporating them into a commercial dataset/model offering.
- Users retain their rights in uploaded data and authored content. This repository license is not a claim over their data or a substitute for the hosted service's privacy notice.
- Linked academic papers and the legacy paper system have their own terms.

Some historical corpus/example records have source attribution without a complete redistribution license determination. [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) makes those limits explicit. Do not infer permission from public availability or from a repository's license for its tooling.

Review the particular source revision, bundled dependencies, datasets/models, required notices, and any employer/institutional rights. Obtain legal advice for the actual product and distribution arrangement; this guide is an engineering explanation of the selected license, not a legal opinion or a guarantee that every third-party asset is cleared.
