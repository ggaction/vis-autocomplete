Privacy and data use

Updated October 26, 2026

VisAutocomplete helps you create and save visualizations. This notice describes account data, private saved work, optional research history, and reports. Contact Hyeon Jeon at jeonhyun97@gmail.com with questions or requests about your data.

Your workspace layout, panel sizes and visibility, preview mode, and code font size are saved as private account preferences so they can be restored when you return or sign in on another device. They are used to operate your workspace, independently of optional research collection.

Accounts and sign-in

Supabase provides authentication. Email accounts use your email address and password through Supabase Auth. Google sign-in requests your basic profile, email address, and account identifier to establish your account. It does not request access to Google Drive, Gmail messages, or your contacts. Authentication sessions are retained in browser storage so you can stay signed in.

Your private work

“Try without account” creates a guest session with Supabase, without asking for an email or password. Its identifier and session credentials are retained in this browser so you can return to the same draft. Signing in from the guest editor carries your current visualization into a new private project in your account.

Guest work is stored in your browser. When signed in, your projects, visualization programs, edit revisions, and datasets are saved privately in Supabase to let you reopen and continue your work. Edit revisions can include earlier states and discarded undo branches. These records and dataset contents are not used as research data.

PNG, PDF, and SVG export sends the chart specification and its included values to the application server for rendering. The export handler processes this transiently and does not persist the chart body.

Optional research history

When you first start a guest session or create an account, you choose whether to share interaction history for research on visualization authoring. The initial checkbox is selected, but collection starts only after you confirm your choice. You can use the editor without opting in and change your choice in Settings.

With permission, we record recommendations generated and actually shown, option and parameter exploration, selections and committed edits, searches, history navigation, direct manipulation, chart structure, timing, error categories, and application/model versions. Research records use a pseudonymous participant identifier, with the account mapping stored separately under restricted access, and include whether they came from a guest. Guest history is pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous. Research history includes field names, chart text (including titles, annotations and categorical labels), original search terms, and structured design arguments. Uploaded files, dataset rows, filter values, authentication information and raw error messages are excluded. This expanded interaction-history notice applies to existing opt-ins; existing opt-outs remain excluded. The same single research preference controls collection. Authorized research administrators can access the history.

Turning off research sharing stops future collection and discards queued events. It does not automatically delete previously shared history. Use “Delete my research history” in Settings to delete server-side research events and stop sharing; this does not delete private projects. Contact us for requests involving previously exported research datasets.

Problem reports

Reports contain the text you submit. If you include project context, the report can contain your visualization code, field names, and chart text. Dataset rows are excluded unless you separately choose to attach the dataset. Report administrators can view reports and any attachments you explicitly share. Reports are separate from the research history.

Service providers and security

Render hosts the application; Supabase provides authentication, database, and private file storage; Resend delivers account confirmation and recovery emails. Google handles Google sign-in when you choose it. These providers process the information needed for their services, including routine connection, delivery, and security logs. The application uses HTTPS and database access rules that restrict private work to its owner. Operators with database administration privileges can access hosted data for support and operation; those privileges do not authorize using private work for research.

Retention and your choices

New detailed research history has a 90-day retention policy, enforced by the maintenance task configured during release. Pending browser research batches expire after 7 days and are capped at 8 MiB. Older research events and exported analysis copies require separate cleanup. This policy does not apply to private saved work or problem reports. Saved work remains until deleted or removed by an administrator. Project deletion removes that project and its revisions; reusable datasets are managed separately in My datasets. Browser-local copies and provider backups or logs may remain separately. Contact us for account, dataset, or research-history deletion requests. Signing out does not erase all browser-local work; on a shared device, clear site data after ensuring your work has synced.

We will update this notice when the service's data handling changes. The single research-sharing preference remains available in Settings. Changes to what is collected are described here and in the in-app notice.
